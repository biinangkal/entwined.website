OUR STORY

Every big idea begins with a deep curiosity - discovering unique hobbies in turning ourselves to be more productive in a fun and creative way.

Crochet is a one-of-a-kind special skill to learn in creating beautiful handcrafted masterpieces, whether you are a starter or not, our mission is to help and gather more people to indulge with this artistic craft. That's how ENTWINED is made.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Hi! I'm Shania, the heart behind ENTWINED.

As a long-time artist myself and a Computer Science student, there can be days where it can feel long and mundane. As I have tried every media of art such as painting, sketching, and doodling, there's always one skill that I'm longing to learn which is crocheting.

In creating this website, I try to incorporate my artistic self and my curiosity in the world of crochet, where it can be a fun hobby to have. In making the name of this website, I searched synonyms for crochet, which is interweave, or intertwined, hence the birth of "ENTWINED".

- Shania, founder of ENTWINED
